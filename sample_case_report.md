# Hypothetical Complaint Investigation Report

**Case ID:** TRAIN-CASE-001  
**Category:** Account information dispute  
**Risk priority:** Medium  
**Status:** Resolved (illustrative)

## Complaint allegation
A fictional consumer reports that information displayed in a servicing portal does not match a previously received statement. The consumer requests clarification and correction if the record is wrong.

## Scope and evidence examined
- Sample portal view (fabricated)
- Sample statement record (fabricated)
- Synthetic workflow log documenting a recent manual update

No authentic customer information was used.

## Investigation steps
1. Confirm the scope of the information difference.
2. Compare the portal and statement fields for the same fictional period.
3. Review the simulated change log to identify when the portal record was updated.
4. Review whether a second-person verification step exists and was documented.

## Findings
The simulated discrepancy resulted from a manual entry error. A second-person verification step was not documented for the change. This constitutes an illustrative process-control gap, **not a legal conclusion**.

## Root cause
Incomplete verification during manual record updates.

## Recommended corrective actions
- Correct the fictional displayed value and verify its consistency with the statement.
- Require review of manual changes to sensitive financial fields.
- Maintain a documented change log and exception report.
- Spot-check a sample of future changes after the process update.

## Communication and closure
Create an accurate, plain-language response explaining the verified result and any corrective action, subject to applicable firm templates, reviews and regulations. Record the date of the response and evidence of closure.

## Escalation trigger
Escalate if similar discrepancies recur, if records remain inconsistent, or if a potential consumer-protection issue is identified.
