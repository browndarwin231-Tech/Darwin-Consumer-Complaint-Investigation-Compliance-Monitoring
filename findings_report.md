# Monthly Complaint Monitoring Report — Sample Dataset

**Period:** September–October 2026 (fictional exercise)  
**Prepared for:** Training portfolio  
**Scope:** 30 synthetic cases

## Executive summary
The exercise register contains **30 complaints**, of which **19 are resolved** and **11 remain open or under review**. **Six of the resolved cases** were closed after their hypothetical internal response targets. These targets are invented for tracking practice and do not establish legal non-compliance.

| Category | Count |
| --- | ---: |
| Account information dispute | 6 |

| **Total** | **30** |

## Monitoring observations
- Account information disputes represent the largest single category in this small fictional sample. Investigate whether data accuracy and change-verification controls are adequate.
- Open and in-review cases merit assignment and deadline monitoring. Review pending cases for any required escalations under actual policies.
- Review late resolutions for patterns in workload allocation, handoffs, evidence gathering and response quality; **do not assume an actual regulatory violation**.

## Recommended corrective action plan

| Action | Example owner | Target | Success measure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Validate change-verification workflow | Operations lead | 30 days | Documented review controls and sample exceptions |
| Review unresolved case queue weekly | Case coordinator | Weekly | Every unresolved case has an owner and next action |

## Limitations
Synthetic data, fabricated processes and illustrative metrics. The analysis is descriptive only and makes no claims about Rausch Sturm LLP, consumer experiences, actual compliance performance or applicable statutory response periods.
